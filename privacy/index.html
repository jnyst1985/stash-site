STASH / PRIVACY

Privacy policy Updated 5 Aug 2026

Privacy

Stash is a link saver that runs on your iPhone. This page is the whole story of what it does with your data. It is short because there is not much to tell.

01 / On your device

Everything you save stays on your device

The links you save, their titles, your notes, your tags and your collections are stored by Stash on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to a server, and I never see them.

02 / Accounts

No accounts

There is no sign-up and no login. Stash never asks for your name, your email address or a password.

03 / Ads

No ads

There are no ads in Stash.

04 / Tracking

No tracking

Stash does not track you. It does not follow you across other apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

05 / Analytics

Anonymized usage analytics

Stash sends a small set of anonymized usage events through TelemetryDeck, so I can see which parts of the app get used. This is the complete list of what is sent.

Every event Stash sends

  • app launched
  • link saved (and whether it came from the app or the share extension)
  • search used
  • export used
  • tag toggled
  • link filed

What is never sent

The content of what you save. An event records that something happened, never a URL, a title, a note, or the name of a tag or a collection. The events are not linked to you or to any account.
